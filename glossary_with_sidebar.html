                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  1.1.1.2      KPI Definitions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  1.1.1.2.1       Mass Loading

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  It refers to when the customer gets the recharge done in the form of Zong load which is any amount above or equal RS.80 or offer/bundle of any domination directly from the retailer, franchise, CSC, mobile appï¿½s, banks and other online platform.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  ï¿½ Conventional recharge:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Recharge from retailer, franchise and CSC fall under this category. Franchise & Sales team KPIs are evaluated on Conventional Recharge only.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Conventional Recharge = Zong Load + VAS (964 & 961) + Scratch Card + Others

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  ï¿½  Zong Load

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Recharge of any denomination transferred from retail to customer. Minimum recharge amount is Rs 80

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  ï¿½  VAS (964 & 961)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Any bundle subscription from retail to customer is fall under VAS recharge.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  *964# menu is used to activate all nationwide bundles

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  *961# menu is used to activate all regional bundles

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Conventional recharge is being calculated based on point in time tagging, it means if a retailer is tagged with Franchise A for 10 days of a month, and 20 days to Franchise B, itï¿½s recharge of 10 Days will be tagged with Franchise A, and recharge of 20 days will be tagged with Franchise B.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Etop (ZL & VAS) is calculated on point in time retail to franchise tagging while Scratch Card is calculated based on latest site to franchise tagging

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  ï¿½ Alternate Recharge:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Recharge from mobile appï¿½s, banking and other online platforms falls under this category.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Alternate recharge is being calculated based on latest site to franchise relation, it means if a retailer is tagged with Franchise A for First 10 days of a month, and Last 20 days to Franchise B, itï¿½s recharge of the month will be tagged with Franchise B (Last tagged).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  1.1.1.2.2       Active ZL Retail Weekly

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Any retailer generating at least two transactions of Etop (Zong Load+VAS) (of minimum denomination) in a period of one week. For KPI calculation average of four weeks of active ZL weekly is being taken.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Absolute Weekly KPI targets is being calculated based on ï¿½Current Tagged Retailersï¿½ which were active as on the last day of previous month multiplies with the target percentage. However, achievement is being calculated based on ï¿½Retailer Transactionï¿½ during the week, and that transactions would be tagged with the latest franchise (last day of each week)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  1.1.1.2.3       Active ZL Retail Monthly

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Any retailer generating at least one transaction of Etop (Zong Load/VAS) (of minimum denomination) in a period of one month.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Absolute Monthly KPI targets are calculated based on ï¿½Active ZL Retail Monthlyï¿½, which represents the retailers active as of the last day of the previous month (base month is locked by S&D at start of every month), However, achievement is calculated based on ï¿½Retailer Transactionsï¿½ during the month, and those transactions are tagged with the latest franchise (as of the last day of the month).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  1.1.1.2.4       Stock Out Retail

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  If available retailer balance at 12pm will be less than 0.50 days of average loading of this particular retailer, the said retailer will be marked as stock outs.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Point in time retailer to franchise tagging is being used to calculate franchise wise KPI. Which means if a retailer is tagged with franchise A for 10 days and with franchise B for 20 days, his performance will be tagged with Franchise A for first 10 days and with Franchise B for next 20 days.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5      FTTH

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5.1.1      Define FTTH

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  FTTH (Fiber to the Home) is a technology that delivers high-speed internet directly to homes or businesses through optical fiber cables. It ensures faster speeds, higher reliability, and better performance compared to traditional copper connections.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5.1.2      What is FTTH-CPN?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  FTTH-CPN (Fiber to the Home ï¿½ Connected Partner Network) is a Zong initiative that enables local fiber operators, who already have deployed fiber infrastructure and are working with other network operators, to collaborate with Zong. Zong provides them bandwidth and a management panel to run and expand their internet services under the Zong umbrella.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5.1.2.1      Who is an Agent?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  An Agent is a partner/operator who already owns or manages a fiber network infrastructure and wishes to use Zongï¿½s bandwidth to serve customers.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Agents operate under their own CVAS (Class Value Added Service) license (A regulatory license that allows telecom operators to provide value-added communication and digital services beyond basic telephony) and are responsible for managing customer operations through the Zong panel.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5.1.2.2      Who is a Sub-Agent?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  A Sub-Agent operates under a main agent.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  Sub-agents can use the main agentï¿½s CVAS license and handle local operations or smaller areas, following the same technical onboarding process with simplified documentation.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5.1.2.3      Target Audience

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  FTTH-CPN focuses on agents who already have deployed fiber infrastructure and are currently working with other network providers. Zong partners with them to provide bandwidth, system access, and operational support through a dedicated management panel.
